# SmartSpend — User Guide

Welcome to **SmartSpend**, your private, encrypted, offline-first personal finance manager.

---

## 1. Quick Start

### First Launch & SMS Permission
1. When you first launch SmartSpend, the app will request permission to read SMS messages.
2. Grant permission so SmartSpend can automatically discover your bank transactions, credit card statements, and FASTag tolls.
3. Tap the **Sync SMS** icon on the top right of the Dashboard.
4. SmartSpend will scan your inbox, extract all financial records, and present your dashboard in seconds.
5. *Note: If testing on an emulator or desktop without an active SIM, go to **Settings** and tap **"Load Golden Sample SMS"** to instantly populate realistic financial data.*

---

## 2. Screen Walkthrough

### 2.1 Dashboard
- **Net Cashflow Hero Banner**: Displays your monthly net cashflow (Total Income minus Total Spend).
- **Bank Balances & Card Spent Cards**: Real-time totals of all detected savings accounts and credit card balances.
- **Alert Banners**: Highlights upcoming credit card bills and transactions that need review.
- **Recent Transactions**: Quick access to your last 10 transactions.

### 2.2 Transactions
- **Search Bar**: Instant full-text search across merchants, categories, and reference IDs.
- **Filter Chips**: Filter by Debits, Card Spends, Credits, Salary, UPI, ATM, or FASTag.
- **Transaction Details**: Tap any transaction to view full details, including masked account numbers, bank name, category, and raw SMS data.

### 2.3 Accounts & Cards
- **Bank Accounts Tab**: Shows current balances for each discovered bank account.
- **Credit Cards Tab**: Displays available credit limits, outstanding amounts, and visual utilization progress bars (alerting when utilization exceeds 30%).

### 2.4 Bills & Statements
- Lists credit card statements with total due, minimum amount due, and countdown to due dates.
- Zero-due or paid bills are marked as "No Payment Required".

### 2.5 Insights & Drill-Down Charts
- **Income vs Spend Chart**: Interactive bar chart comparing cashflow.
- **Category Pie Chart**: Breakdown of spending by category.
- **Drill-Down**: Tap any category slice to immediately filter and view all transactions in that category!

### 2.6 Review Queue
- Low-confidence or unparsed SMS messages appear here for your approval.
- **Edit Details**: Correct merchant names, amounts, or categories.
- **Mark Non-Financial**: Exclude OTPs, marketing SMS, or non-expense messages.
- **Approve**: Confirm parsed values and promote to high-confidence.

### 2.7 Settings & Export
- **Theme Mode**: Switch between AMOLED Dark Mode and Light Mode.
- **Currency**: Select default currency (INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED).
- **Biometric App Lock**: Enable fingerprint or face unlock.
- **Export to JSON / CSV**: Export your entire financial vault anytime.
